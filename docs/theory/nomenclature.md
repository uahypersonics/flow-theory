# Nomenclature

This page collects the most common notation used across the theory notes in this project. Each page still defines any symbol that is used with a local convention or a special meaning.

## General flow quantities

- $M_\infty$: free-stream Mach number.
- $p_\infty$, $T_\infty$, $\rho_\infty$, $u_\infty$: free-stream pressure, temperature, density, and velocity.
- $R$: gas constant.
- $\gamma$: ratio of specific heats.
- $c_p$: constant-pressure specific heat.
- $\Pr$: Prandtl number.
- $\mu$: dynamic viscosity.
- $Re_x = \rho_\infty u_\infty x / \mu_\infty$: Reynolds number based on distance $x$.
- $Re_\theta = \rho_\infty u_e \theta / \mu_e$: Reynolds number based on momentum thickness $\theta$.

## Boundary-layer and wall quantities

- $u_e$: boundary-layer edge velocity.
- $T_e$: boundary-layer edge temperature.
- $T_w$: wall temperature.
- $q_w$: wall heat flux.
- $c_f$: skin-friction coefficient.
- $St$: Stanton number.
- $\delta$: boundary-layer thickness.
- $\theta$: momentum thickness.
- $h_w$: wall enthalpy or wall heat-transfer quantity, depending on the formulation used in a given section.

## Shock and geometry notation

- $x$, $y$: streamwise and normal coordinates.
- $\eta$: similarity variable or transformed wall-normal coordinate.
- $\Delta$: shock stand-off distance.
- $R_n$: nose radius.

## Transition and stability notation

- $\alpha$: streamwise wavenumber or amplification parameter.
- $\beta$: spanwise wavenumber.
- $\omega$: frequency.
- $N$: amplification factor used in $e^N$ transition estimates.

## Notes

- The same symbol may be used differently in different references; the local page should be treated as the authoritative definition for that section.
- This list is intended to grow over time as more theory modules are added.
