# Boundary-Layer Transition Correlations

## Streamwise transition

**$\mathrm{Re}_\theta / M_e$ criterion**

> Berry, S. A., and Horvath, T. J., "Discrete Roughness Transition for Hypersonic Flight Vehicles," *Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets*, Vol. 45, No. 2, 2008, pp. 216–227.

**van Driest–Blumer correlation**

> van Driest, E. R., and Blumer, C. B., "Boundary Layer Transition: Freestream Turbulence and Pressure Gradient Effects," *AIAA Journal*, Vol. 1, No. 6, 1963, pp. 1303–1306.

**Pate–Schueler noise correlation**

> Pate, S. R., and Schueler, C. J., "Radiated Aerodynamic Noise Effects on Boundary-Layer Transition in Supersonic and Hypersonic Wind Tunnels," *AIAA Journal*, Vol. 7, No. 3, 1969, pp. 450–457.

## Crossflow transition

**Crossflow Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re}_{cf}$**

> Owen, P. R., and Randall, D. G., "Boundary Layer Transition on a Sweptback Wing," RAE TM Aero 277, Royal Aircraft Establishment, 1952.
>
> Reed, H. L., and Haynes, T. S., "Transition Correlations in Three-Dimensional Boundary Layers," *AIAA Journal*, Vol. 32, No. 5, 1994, pp. 923–929.

**Arnal C1 criterion**

> Arnal, D., Habiballah, M., and Coustols, E., "Théorie de l'instabilité laminaire et critères de transition en écoulement bi et tridimensionnel," *La Recherche Aérospatiale*, No. 2, 1984, pp. 125–143.

**$\gamma$–$\mathrm{Re}_{\theta t}$ crossflow extensions**

> Grabe, C., and Krumbein, A., "Correlation-Based Transition Transport Modeling for Three-Dimensional Aerodynamic Configurations," *Journal of Aircraft*, Vol. 50, No. 5, 2013, pp. 1533–1539.
>
> Langtry, R. B., Sengupta, K., Yeh, D. T., and Dorgan, A. J., "Extending the $\gamma$-$\overline{\mathrm{Re}_{\theta t}}$ Local Correlation Based Transition Model for Crossflow Effects," AIAA Paper 2015-2474, 2015.

## Nose-bluntness effects

**Stetson blunt-cone correlations**

> Stetson, K. F., "Nosetip Bluntness Effects on Cone Frustum Boundary Layer Transition in Hypersonic Flow," AIAA Paper 83-1763, 1983.

## Roughness-induced transition

**Roughness Reynolds number $\mathrm{Re}_{kk}$**

> Schneider, S. P., "Effects of Roughness on Hypersonic Boundary-Layer Transition," *Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets*, Vol. 45, No. 2, 2008, pp. 193–209.

**PANT-type correlations**

> Reda, D. C., "Review and Synthesis of Roughness-Dominated Transition Correlations for Reentry Applications," *Journal of Spacecraft and Rockets*, Vol. 39, No. 2, 2002, pp. 161–167.

**$e^N$ method**

> Smith, A. M. O., and Gamberoni, N., "Transition, Pressure Gradient and Stability Theory," Report ES 26388, Douglas Aircraft Co., 1956.

> van Ingen, J. L., "A Suggested Semi-Empirical Method for the Calculation of the Boundary Layer Transition Region," Report VTH-74, Delft University of Technology, 1956.

> Mack, L. M., "Boundary-Layer Linear Stability Theory," AGARD Report No. 709, 1984.
